The Gazette is set after dark, when the day can finally be
read whole. Black and fire orange, by lamplight: Tuesday
25 November 2026 in thirty-seven stories from nine regions,
each one dated — today, or with its true date on its face.

The night opens on the heaviest figure of the day. A new
analysis counts 700,000 or more Russian soldiers killed in
the nearly five-year war on Ukraine — the tall lead, its
field of crosses drawn without ornament. Beside it, the two
stories the night could not hold till morning: Baghdad's
order for federal units to seize the Kurdish oilfields and
crossings, and Gaza, where drone strikes killed seven — a
Hamas security chief, three policemen, three siblings, one
of them a child — each death entered by role, as the count
arrived.

Under lamplight, the rest of the day reads in order. Poland,
where NATO is sending early-warning jets after Russian
missiles landed there, with Chancellor Merz's promise of
complete support. Hormuz, where Iran says three tankers were
hit by mines — entered as CLAIMED, in the Gazette's own
hand, because a claim by lamplight is still a claim. Fifty
Boeing jets on a Xiamen order. Twelve coral species brought
back on the Great Barrier Reef. Madrid's 5–1 comeback.
Brussels' new sanctions. Storms closing on Thanksgiving
travel. Seven firms targeted over chip diversion. And the
PCE report, due this day, its line left open — no figure had
landed by the time the lamps went out, and the Gazette does
not invent one.

Yesterday's news is named at the foot of the page and kept
out of tonight's: a Mercury story dated 23 November, climate
talks from earlier in the month, a Liverpool result from an
earlier round, launch hype for a game with no release today.
Nvidia's rise reads under its true colours — MONDAY CLOSE,
a Monday story wearing Monday's date. Indonesia's
vice-presidential resignation rides in the numbered briefs,
as a single-source report, nothing added.

For the night reader: the day's eight heaviest figures run
along the top, each tagged with its card. The night map is
pinned by card number. The night watch reads the day from
Monday's close to midnight. Region counts cover only
stories on the page.

Set two ways in the Gazette office: `compose_midnight.py`
lays the page out under a fire band with three leads abreast,
and again under a paper masthead — tall lead at left, the two
urgent stories stacked beside it; the duel is kept with the
build notes, and the masthead setting stands. `main.py`
carries the finished page to `/output/entry.png` byte for
byte.

Wires: RFE/RL, Al Jazeera, DW, Starlit Daily, Forbes.
Drawings original to this gazette.
